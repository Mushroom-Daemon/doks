### A character

Link: writing a customer persona

  * Who are they?
  * What do they want? Whyx5
  * What problems do they face?
  * What’s their level of awareness?
  * What do they need to feel comfortable buying?
  * What do they value?

A homeowner—typically 55 years or older and able to pay cash—wants to add solar to their home. This may be to fulfill a long-time goal and is seen as a final home improvement project. Generally, they value ownership, predictability, and passing on assets to the next generation. They come from a variety of awareness levels. Some just want solar; some know the systems. All want power security.

### Has a problem

These homeowners don’t have a pressing problem, per se, but do face the same realities as the general public: rising energy costs, an unstable grid, and AI data centers consuming massive amounts of energy.
