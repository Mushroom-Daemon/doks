Lead Generator PDF Notes


Starts the relationship and teaches them how you solve their problem

They are signing up to get your sales ptich

Speak directly to the person/identity that you wish to ultimately sell to

Get their contact information otherwise both of you might be a little dissapointed

A good lead generator should:

1. Position your biz as the guide (empathy/authority = you know their problems and can solve them)
2.	Stake claim to your territory
3.	Qualify your audience
4.	Create trust by solving a problem!!! (the air thing teaches them how to play high notes – easier
5.	Create reciprocity (how can you create enormous value for them?)
6.	Get an email address
7.	Always use an interesting title: catchy and bold . . . fascinations are good!

Lead Generator Ideas:

»	Checklists
»	Host interview w/ industry expert
»	Reusable worksheet
»	Host a free giveaway (a service)
»	The sampler
»	Webinars
»	Give a keynote
»	Value list (5 ways to . . . )
»	Pitfall list (five mistakes to avoid . . . )
»	Have an open house
Lead Gen PDF tips:
»	Don't focus on too many problems (pick on thing – be focused – it just shows you're good and solving problems)
»	Less text is better
»	Keep language clear and concise – be specific
»	Use a clear and catchy title (the VALUE that people get)

Effective PDF Content:

1.	Introduce the problem
2.	Ramp up the prob
3.	Deliver value (title)
4.	Vision of success and CTA

More tips: make it scannable

Don't overthink it

Not too much text

It never hurts to be known as a generous brand – it creates reciprocity

Give away a lot and then sell the "I'll hold your hand now"

If a picture's worth 1,000 words, a video's worth, like, a lot more than 1,000.

Statistic: get a percentage into a fraction that is less than one, example: 7/8 vs 87%

"Five things your website should include"
"Skyrocket your profit"
"top ten questions every parent should ask on a private school tour"
"the unwritten rules that will make or break your firefighter interview"
"5 ways other lenders rip you off"
The first thing to do is come up with a GOOD TITLE!
